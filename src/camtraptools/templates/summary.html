Methods and limitations

  • RAI compares how often species are recorded; it is not a measure of density, because species differ in how easily cameras record them.
  • Occupancy estimates with a warning are unreliable: the species was recorded too rarely, or at too few sites, to separate rarity from being hard to detect.
  • AI labels contain mistakes, especially for night images and rare species; species never recorded may be present.
  • Detection histories, abundance indices, activity curves and the full model results are produced by camtraptools ecology.
